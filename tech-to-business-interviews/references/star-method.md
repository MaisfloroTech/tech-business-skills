# STAR deep dive

Every question in the guide is evaluated with **STAR**. The interviewer doesn't need the candidate to say the letters; they need to **hear** all four, and to dig until they do.

| Letter | What the candidate should give | Listen for | Dig deeper if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| **S** · Situation | The real context: company type, system, scale, why it mattered | A concrete case, not "usually we…" | "When was this? What was at stake for the business?" |
| **T** · Task | **Their** responsibility and the goal | "I owned…", a target, a deadline | "What exactly was yours to solve? What did success look like?" |
| **A** · Action | What **they** did and **why**: decisions, trade-offs, who they involved | "I decided… because…", options discarded | "What else did you consider? Why not that? Who did you have to convince?" |
| **R** · Result | What changed, **in numbers**, and what it meant for the business | Before → after, cost, customers, risk, time | "What number moved? What did that mean for customers or money? What would you do differently?" |

## The deep dive

One answer is never enough. A good interviewer goes **two or three levels down** on the same story:

1. **First level:** the story as the candidate tells it.
2. **Second level:** one specific decision. *"Why that and not the alternative?"*
3. **Third level:** the business behind it. *"How did you know it was worth it? Who outside engineering noticed?"*

Real experience gets **more** specific as you dig. Rehearsed or borrowed stories get vaguer.

## Red flags

- **"We" all the time and never "I".** Ask: *"What was your part?"*
- **Hypothetical instead of real.** *"I would…"* when the question asked for a time they did it. Ask for a real case.
- **No result, or a result with no number.** *"It went well."* Ask what number moved.
- **Only technical result.** *"Latency went down."* Ask what it meant for customers or the business.
- **Blames others for every problem.** Ask what they would do differently.

## Scoring (1–4)

Score each question right after the answer, while it's fresh.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **4 · Strong** | Full STAR, real case, own actions and reasoning, result in numbers **and** its business meaning. Holds up under the deep dive. |
| **3 · Good** | Full STAR with a real case; the business meaning or the numbers are partial. |
| **2 · Partial** | Missing one or two letters (usually A or R), or stays technical with no business link. |
| **1 · Weak** | Generic or hypothetical, no own actions, no result. Falls apart when you dig. |

Mark which STAR letters you heard (S, T, A, R) next to each question. A missing letter after digging is information, not a reason to keep pushing forever.
