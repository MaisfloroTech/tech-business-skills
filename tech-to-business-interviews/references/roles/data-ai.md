# Data & AI

People who turn data into decisions, features and models. Their business levers: **better or faster decisions, trusted data, and AI features that actually create value.**

Family-wide ladder:

| Step | Ask for |
|---|---|
| Output | Dashboards, pipelines, analyses, models, datasets, AI features |
| Outcome | Who uses it and how often, data freshness and quality, decisions it informed, model accuracy in production, adoption of AI features |
| Business impact | Revenue or cost effect of the decision taken, hours of manual work removed, risk detected earlier, conversion/retention from data-driven features |

---

### Data Analyst
- **Produces:** analyses and answers to business questions.
- **Serves:** business teams and leadership.
- **If it stops:** decisions are made on intuition, or wait.
- **Ask especially for:** the decision each analysis informed and what happened after it; time to answer a request.
- **Typical risks:** conclusions from incomplete or inconsistent data, too many ad-hoc requests to do deep work.
- **Red flag:** "delivered 20 analyses" without a single decision traced to them.

### BI Analyst
- **Produces:** dashboards, reports and metric definitions.
- **Serves:** managers and leadership who track the business.
- **If it stops:** nobody agrees on the numbers; meetings argue about data instead of decisions.
- **Ask especially for:** active users of dashboards, reports retired, time saved in manual reporting, metric definitions agreed.
- **Typical risks:** two dashboards showing different numbers for the same metric, broken source data.
- **Red flag:** counting dashboards built instead of dashboards used.

### Data Engineer
- **Produces:** pipelines, data platforms, datasets others can trust.
- **Serves:** analysts, data scientists, products and finance.
- **If it stops:** data is late, wrong or missing; everyone downstream stalls.
- **Ask especially for:** pipeline success rate, data freshness vs SLA, data quality incidents, cost of the data platform, time to onboard a new source.
- **Typical risks:** silent data quality issues, cost growth of the warehouse, a fragile pipeline behind a critical report.
- **Red flag:** "migrated pipelines" without freshness, reliability or cost results.

### AI and Data Scientist / Machine Learning
- **Produces:** models, experiments, predictions.
- **Serves:** products and business processes that use predictions.
- **If it stops:** models go stale; opportunities found in data are never used.
- **Ask especially for:** model performance **in production** (not only offline), business metric moved by the model (e.g. fraud caught, churn prevented, conversion), experiment results with confidence.
- **Typical risks:** model drift, bias or fairness issues, a model nobody owns in production.
- **Red flag:** reporting accuracy or AUC without the business metric it changed.

### AI Engineer
- **Produces:** features built on AI models: assistants, agents, retrieval, automations.
- **Serves:** end users and internal teams.
- **If it stops:** AI features stay as demos and never create value.
- **Ask especially for:** adoption and retention of the feature, task success rate, human time saved, cost per interaction, quality/error rate reviewed by humans.
- **Typical risks:** cost per use growing, hallucinations reaching customers, provider dependency, data privacy.
- **Red flag:** "launched an AI assistant" with no usage, quality or cost numbers.
