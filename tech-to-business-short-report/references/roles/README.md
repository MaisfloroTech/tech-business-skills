# Roles

The same "impact" question means different things for a developer, a security engineer or a developer advocate. Knowing the role lets the assistant ask concrete questions instead of generic ones.

Roles are grouped into **families** that share the same business levers. Role names follow the role-based roadmaps on [roadmap.sh](https://roadmap.sh/roadmaps), plus a few common roles that are not listed there. Definitions here are our own; for the skills each role needs, see roadmap.sh.

## Always ask for a one-line role definition

Even when the role is on the list. Titles mean different things in different companies, and some real roles are not listed at all (support engineer, IT operations, solutions architect, customer engineer...).

Ask the writer three things:

1. **What do you produce?**
2. **Who uses it?**
3. **What breaks, and for whom, if you stop doing it for a month?**

The third answer is almost always the business impact. If the role is not in any family, build a card on the fly from those three answers and use the closest family as reference.

## Card format

Each role card has:

- **Produces / Serves / If it stops** — the one-line definition.
- **Ladder** — what to ask for at each step (output → outcome → business impact).
- **Typical risks** — what usually goes in a RISK item for this role.
- **Red flag** — the most common way people in this role under-report their impact.
