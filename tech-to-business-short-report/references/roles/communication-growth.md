# Communication & Growth

People who make products understood, findable and adopted. Their business levers: **reach to the right audience, adoption, and less friction (fewer questions, faster onboarding).**

Family-wide ladder:

| Step | Ask for |
|---|---|
| Output | Articles, talks, docs, workshops, videos, pages optimized |
| Outcome | Reach **within the target audience**, engagement, sign-ups/activations, docs usage, search ranking and traffic |
| Business impact | Product adoption, pipeline influenced, cost of acquisition, support tickets avoided, onboarding time reduced |

---

### Developer Relations
- **Produces:** talks, articles, sample code, workshops, community programs.
- **Serves:** developers who could use the product, and the product team (feedback).
- **If it stops:** fewer developers discover, try and stay with the product; product loses a feedback channel.
- **Ask especially for:** audience size **and** fit (who attended, not just how many), sign-ups or activations attributed, community growth and activity, product feedback that changed the roadmap.
- **Typical risks:** activity with no measurable adoption, dependence on one event or platform, attribution nobody trusts.
- **Red flag:** "gave 12 talks" without who was in the room and what they did afterwards.

### Technical Writer
- **Produces:** documentation, guides, API references, release notes.
- **Serves:** users, developers, support and onboarding.
- **If it stops:** users can't self-serve; support and engineering answer the same questions repeatedly.
- **Ask especially for:** docs page views and search success, support tickets on documented topics before/after, time to first successful API call, feedback ratings.
- **Typical risks:** docs out of date after a release, missing docs for a launching feature.
- **Red flag:** "published 40 pages" without tickets avoided or task success.

### SEO
- **Produces:** findability: technical SEO, content optimization, site structure.
- **Serves:** marketing, growth, product.
- **If it stops:** organic traffic declines; acquisition gets more expensive.
- **Ask especially for:** organic traffic to target pages, rankings for target terms, organic conversions, cost of equivalent paid traffic.
- **Typical risks:** search engine algorithm changes, site migrations losing traffic, technical errors de-indexing pages.
- **Red flag:** reporting rankings without traffic or conversions.
